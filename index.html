About
Emmet Wall is a singer and drummer from central New Jersey whose music draws from hard rock, new wave, and metal. He began his musical education at School of Rock in Princeton at age six, developing a foundation that shaped both his technical ability and his instinct for songwriting.

His debut album AT (2023) arrived when Emmet was fifteen — a record that blended hard rock energy with new wave sensibility, its lyrics shaped by the particular strangeness of adolescence during the COVID years.

His second album Deep Walls marks a deliberate turn toward heavier territory. The first single Adem's Song (December 2025) established the new direction, followed by Fools (January 2026) and Gravitron In My Head (February 2026). A fourth single ZRHD was released June 2026.
